Infer mitochondrial lineage tree from Himito read_var matrix CSV and heteroplasmic variants

Usage: Himito lineage [OPTIONS] --matrix-file <MATRIX_FILE> --output-prefix <OUTPUT_PREFIX>

Options:
  -m, --matrix-file <MATRIX_FILE>
          Himito matrix CSV (<prefix>.matrix.csv)

      --threads <THREADS>
          worker threads for parallel stages

  -v, --vcf-file <VCF_FILE>
          Himito VCF; restricts the analysis to its heteroplasmic sites

  -d, --data-type <DATA_TYPE>
          sequencing data type; sets the SCITE error-rate preset
          
          [default: pacbio]
          [possible values: pacbio, ont-r9, ont-r10, ont-denoised]

  -o, --output-prefix <OUTPUT_PREFIX>
          output prefix; writes <prefix>.haplotype_map.tsv and the trees

      --min-hf <MIN_HF>
          minimum heteroplasmic frequency (inclusive) of a variant
          
          [default: 0.01]

      --max-hf <MAX_HF>
          maximum heteroplasmic frequency (exclusive); excludes fixed variants
          
          [default: 0.95]

      --fp-rate <FP_RATE>
          SCITE false-positive rate (preset by data type)

      --fn-rate <FN_RATE>
          SCITE false-negative rate for SNVs (default: estimated per sample from near-fixed SNVs, floor 0.005; data-type preset when too few)

      --hp-fp-rate <HP_FP_RATE>
          SCITE false-positive rate for HP-tract alleles (default: D310/HVS1-C from the hp_confusion.tsv table; CA514 twice --fp-rate or its preset)

      --hp-fn-rate <HP_FN_RATE>
          SCITE false-negative rate for HP-tract alleles (default: D310/HVS1-C from the hp_confusion.tsv table; CA514 twice --fn-rate or its preset)

      --hp-error <HP_ERROR>
          hp_confusion.tsv (`Himito hp-error`) for D310/HVS1-C allele rates [default: <X>.hp_confusion.tsv beside <X>.matrix.csv, else the embedded HG00097 table]

      --tract-alleles <TRACT_ALLELES>
          D310/HVS1-C alleles: site = alleles at one tract position are one categorical site whose states may recur on several branches; auto = site, except ONT alleles beyond the table's calibrated run length are annotate-only (any --hp-*-rate: binary rows; both: those ONT alleles are searched too); search = one binary row per allele; annotate
          
          [default: auto]
          [possible values: auto, site, search, annotate]

      --collapse-hp
          merge equivalent spellings inside known rCRS homopolymer tracts

      --mcmc-iterations <MCMC_ITERATIONS>
          MCMC iterations per chain
          
          [default: 5000]

      --mcmc-chains <MCMC_CHAINS>
          independent MCMC chains; the best tree is kept
          
          [default: 3]

      --debug
          also write raw_haplotype_map.tsv, raw_variant_cooccurrence.tsv and cleaned_variant_cooccurrence.tsv

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
